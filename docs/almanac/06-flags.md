## The Jolly Roger
Under the {item:pirates_n_ships:jolly_roger_flag}**Jolly Roger** the pirates let you be and merchants may strike to you. The navy fires on everyone aboard on sight, and being seen under it is a crime.
{recipe:jolly_roger_flag}

## The flagpole
A {item:pirates_n_ships:flagpole}**flagpole** flies the flag downwind from its top, a block high and a block and a half long, so leave it room.
{recipe:flagpole}
- **Use it with a flag:** the flag flies after three seconds; you get the old one back.
- **Empty hand:** strike the colours, or raise them again.
- **Sneak, empty hand:** take the flag down.

## Tall poles
Stack flagpoles into one pole up to 6 blocks tall. The top flies the flag, and you may work it from any block.

A {item:pirates_n_ships:flag_pedestal}**flag pedestal** is a sturdy foot: set it on solid ground and stand a pole in it. Breaking it brings the whole pole down.
{recipe:flag_pedestal}

## False colours
A ship flies the highest flag on her poles. Under the navy flag you pass the navy while your record is clean, but a suspect may be found out by any navy soldier in sight. Then you are charged heavily and hunted for five minutes.

>! Firing on struck colours, a merchant flag or a banner is a crime, and your crew's shots are charged to you.

## Striking your colours
Strike your flag to surrender: navy and pirates stop attacking. Raise the Jolly Roger again and the chase is back on. See [[pirates_n_ships:law|Law]].

## Dyed sails
Use a dye on the upper yard of a square sail, or the head cleat of a triangular one, to colour the cloth; white dye brings back plain canvas. A large square sail takes a banner the same way.
Colours are only for show.
