The Crown keeps a ledger on every soul at sea. Stay honest and the navy is your friend; turn pirate and you had better be fast.

## Your record
Every crime adds to your record: theft, violence, plunder aboard, false colours and, worst of all, piracy and desertion.

From 10 you are a **suspect**, from 50 **wanted**, from 200 **notorious**. Keep clean and it fades day by day.

> Theft counts only when someone sees it.

## Fines and bounties
Right-click a navy officer with doubloons to pay off your record. He will not deal with a wanted man, and no coin buys a pardon for the notorious.

A wanted man carries a bounty. Kill him and bring the {item:pirates_n_ships:bounty_proof}**bounty proof** to a navy officer; alive pays more.

## Prisoners
- A prisoner stops fighting and never wanders off.
- Use the shackles on it again to lead it, or to let go.
- Left alone outside a cell, it tries to slip away.

Right-click a navy officer with an empty hand, prisoners close by, to turn them in or ransom them. Your shackles come back.

## Press-ganging
Right-click a shackled sailor on your own ship with the {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_whistle}**captain's whistle** to press-gang him into your crew. He comes aboard sullen, and it is a crime.

Sneak-use your own prisoner with an empty hand to set him free.

## The three powers
The navy, the pirates and the villagers each remember you, from −100 to 100.

- Killing pirates pleases the navy; plunder pleases the pirates; trade pleases the villagers.
- Good standing means better prices. Villagers stop trading at −60.
- Liked pirates leave you be until you strike first. At −60 the navy fires on sight.

## Navy patrols
Navy patrols chase the Jolly Roger, false colours they see through, and wanted men.

Strike your colours and their guns fall silent. Outrun them and they give up.

## Careers
You may climb only one ladder.

- **The navy:** enlist with an officer once the navy trusts you, and rise from Midshipman to Admiral.
- **Infamy:** plunder and taken ships make you a Buccaneer, a Dread Captain and a Pirate Lord.
- **Letter of marque:** bought from a navy officer; pirates you kill earn prize money.

## Rewards of rank
- **Lieutenant:** the navy flag, free docking at navy outposts, and an officer's uniform and saber.
- **Navy Captain:** a {item:pirates_n_ships:ship_commission}**ship commission** for an armed sloop, and cheaper ships at navy desks.
- **Dread Captain:** a pirate sloop from a pirate island's fence.

Your title rides before your name in chat and over your head.
