Every block and item a hand may need, a line on each, and the chapter that tells the rest.

## The ship herself
- {item:pirates_n_ships:helm}**Helm:** launches, steers and takes apart a ship. [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:yard}**Yard:** a spar; two on a mast make a square sail. [[pirates_n_ships:sailing|Sailing]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:cleat}**Cleat:** holds a stay, a rope line or a grappling rope.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:rope}**Rope:** rigs stays and rope lines.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:sail_winch}**Sail winch:** works every sail aboard. A station.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:capstan}**Capstan:** drops and weighs the anchor. A station.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:mooring_ring}**Mooring ring:** catches and holds a grappling hook.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:ratlines}**Ratlines:** rope nets for climbing aloft.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:crows_nest}**Crow's nest:** the lookout's barrel. A station.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:ships_bell}**Ship's bell:** rings when struck.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:bilge_pump}**Bilge pump:** pumps a flooded hold. A station.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:hull_patch}**Hull patch:** closes a breach.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:flagpole}**Flagpole:** flies your colours. [[pirates_n_ships:flags|Flags]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:flag_pedestal}**Flag pedestal:** a carved plinth a flagpole stands in.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:nameplate}**Name Board:** shows her name across the boards it joins.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:figurehead_mermaid}{item:pirates_n_ships:figurehead_lion}{item:pirates_n_ships:figurehead_eagle}{item:pirates_n_ships:figurehead_skull}**Figureheads:** a mermaid, lion, eagle or skull for the bow.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:ship_lantern}**Ship's lantern:** a lantern that burns under water.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:stern_window}**Stern window:** shuttered windows for the great cabin.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:rope_coil}**Rope coil:** coiled rope for the deck.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:chart_table}**Chart table:** a captain's table.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_cot}**Sea cot:** the captain's bed at sea.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:hammock}**Hammock:** a bunk for one hand. [[pirates_n_ships:crew|Crew]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:boarding_plank}**Boarding plank:** a bridge to an enemy deck.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:oak_railing}{item:pirates_n_ships:oak_railing_port}**Railing, railing port:** a rail along a block's edge; click again to rail another. A cannon fires and a boarding plank goes out through the port.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:oak_railing_gate}**Railing gate:** a railing section that swings open like a fence gate (use it, or redstone), so crew and boarding planks pass when it is open; a lantern hangs below or stands on any railing post, and a chain runs on from it.

## Stores and cargo
- {item:pirates_n_ships:pantry}**Pantry:** the food store. [[pirates_n_ships:provisions|Provisions]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:water_barrel}**Water barrel:** 16 rations of fresh water.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:cargo_crate}**Cargo crate:** 32 stacks of one item. [[pirates_n_ships:trade|Cargo and trade]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:cargo_barrel}**Cargo barrel:** 1536 items of one kind.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_chest}**Sea chest:** 54 stacks that float.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:paddle}**Paddle:** rows a floating sea chest.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:hardtack}**Hardtack,** {item:pirates_n_ships:salted_fish}**salted fish,** {item:pirates_n_ships:salt_pork}**salt pork:** food that never spoils.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:lime}**Lime:** fruit against scurvy.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:rum}**Rum:** cheers a crew and warms a swimmer.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:doubloon}**Doubloon:** the coin of the sea.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:tobacco}**Tobacco,** {item:pirates_n_ships:spices}**spices,** {item:pirates_n_ships:cloth}**cloth:** trade goods.

## Arms and shot
- {item:pirates_n_ships:rapier}**Rapier,** {item:pirates_n_ships:cutlass}**cutlass,** {item:pirates_n_ships:saber}**saber:** swords. [[pirates_n_ships:combat|Weapons and combat]]

## Law and command
- {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_whistle}**Captain's whistle:** orders the crew.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:harbor_desk}**Harbour master's desk:** a port's market.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:notice_board}**Notice board:** every bounty at sea. [[pirates_n_ships:law|Law]]
- {item:pirates_n_ships:bounty_proof}**Bounty proof:** proof of a kill; hand it to a navy officer.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:shackles}**Shackles:** take a beaten foe prisoner.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:brig_bars}**Brig bars,** {item:pirates_n_ships:brig_door}**brig door,** {item:pirates_n_ships:brig_key}**brig key:** the brig.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:merchant_flag}**Merchant flag,** {item:pirates_n_ships:navy_flag}**navy flag,** {item:pirates_n_ships:jolly_roger_flag}**Jolly Roger:** your colours.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:ship_receipt}**Ship receipt:** a shipwright's promise.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:ship_commission}**Ship commission:** an armed sloop earned by rank.
