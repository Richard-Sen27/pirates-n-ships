| Block | Recipe | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:helm" /> | 4 sticks, 1 planks | Assembles, steers and disassembles a ship. A real ship's wheel on a pedestal (Blockbench model). See [Ships](ships.md) and [Sailing](sailing.md). |
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:yard" /> | 3 logs in a row (gives 3) | A spar. Two rows on one mast make a square sail; the cloth is drawn between them. See [Sails](sailing.md#sails). |
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:cleat" /> | iron ingot, planks | Attaches to floors, walls and masts. Three cleats and a rope make a triangular sail. See [Sails](sailing.md#sails). |
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:sail_winch" /> | 2 string, 1 iron ingot, 3 planks | Sets the trim of all sails on its ship. A crew station. The crank faces you when placed. |
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:capstan" /> | 2 logs, 1 stick, 2 chains, 1 iron block, 3 planks | Drops and raises the anchor. |
| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:harbor_desk" /> | book, gold nugget, 5 planks | Opens a port's market screen when bound to the port. See [Harbor master's desk](trade.md#harbor-master's-desk). |

| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:bilge_pump" /> | stick, 3 planks, 1 bucket, 1 plank | Pumps water out of the hold below it. A crew station. See [Fighting a leak](ships.md#fighting-a-leak). |

| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:water_barrel" /> | 6 planks, 2 iron nuggets, 1 water bucket | Holds 16 rations of water. Crafted full. |

| <ItemLink id="pirates_n_ships:brig_bars" /> | 4 iron bars, 2 planks (gives 6) | Bars for cells. |

Click the hull block it should hang on: the plate lands there and the figure looks at you. |

Server option `ship_identity.nameplate_shows_name`. |

All blocks drop themselves. Wooden ones are mined with an axe, the bars and the door with a pickaxe.

## Hats
Wear a pirate hat, bandana, navy tricorn or officer's bicorne by right-clicking with it or putting it in the
helmet slot. Each gives +1 armour (server config `apparel.hat_armor`; 0 turns it off). Crafted from three black wool
over leather, bone, leather (pirate hat), leather, white wool, leather (navy tricorn) or leather, gold nugget, leather
(officer's bicorne); red wool, string, red wool for the bandana.
