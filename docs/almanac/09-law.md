The Crown keeps a ledger on every soul at sea, and on every beast that bites. Steal, strike or sail under false colours and your name goes in it. Stay honest and the navy is your friend; turn pirate and you had better be fast.

## Your record
Every crime adds to your record:

- Theft from a village chest, or striking a villager: 5.
- Striking the navy, or being seen under the Jolly Roger: 10.
- Attacking a neutral ship, press-ganging a prisoner, or being caught with plunder aboard: 15.
- Offering stolen goods at a village or navy desk: 17.
- Killing a villager: 20. Killing a navy man: 30.
- Caught under false colours, or firing on a ship that struck: 40.
- Piracy (taking a ship): 50. Deserting the navy: 60.

From 10 you are a **suspect**, from 50 **wanted**, and from 200 **notorious**. Five minutes after your last crime your record begins to fade, by 10 a day.

> Theft counts only when someone sees it: a villager, a trader or a navy man nearby. Putting things into a chest is no crime.

## Fines
Hold doubloons and right-click a navy officer to pay your fine: 3 doubloons a point, as many whole points as you can afford. He will not deal with a wanted man, and no coin buys a pardon for the notorious.

## Bounties
At a record of 50 the navy puts a price on your head of twice your record. It grows as your record grows and is withdrawn once the record falls below 25.

Kill someone with a price on their head and a {item:pirates_n_ships:bounty_proof}**bounty proof** lands in your pack. Right-click a navy officer with it to collect. Bring the quarry in alive and it pays half again as much. A claim wipes the quarry's record clean.

## The notice board
illustration: notice_board
The {item:pirates_n_ships:notice_board}**notice board** (planks and paper) lists every bounty: who is wanted, for how much, who placed it and when, and whether there is one on you.

To place a bounty of your own, type a name, enter at least 10 doubloons and press **Place**. The coin comes out of your pack.

## Shackles and prisoners
illustration: shackles
{item:pirates_n_ships:shackles}**Shackles** (two iron ingots and a chain) take a beaten foe alive.

- Use them on a creature at a quarter of its health or less: it becomes your prisoner. It stops fighting and never wanders off. Bosses cannot be taken, and players only where the server allows it and they carry a bounty.
- Use them on your prisoner to lead it, or to let go. A led prisoner follows like a beast on a lead.
- A prisoner left alone outside a cell tries to slip away.

## Turning in prisoners
Walk up to a navy officer with your prisoners close by and right-click him with an empty hand.

- A prisoner with a bounty pays half again the bounty; a pirate earns a reward besides.
- A shackled navy man or merchant can be ransomed back the same way.
- You get your shackles back.

Hold the {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_whistle}**captain's whistle** and right-click a shackled sailor on your own ship to press-gang him into your crew. He comes aboard sullen, and it is a crime. Sneak and right-click your own prisoner with an empty hand to set him free.

## The brig
illustration: brig_door
{item:pirates_n_ships:brig_bars}**Brig bars** connect like iron bars. The {item:pirates_n_ships:brig_door}**brig door** locks with a {item:pirates_n_ships:brig_key}**brig key** (an iron ingot over an iron nugget): use the key on the door to lock it, and again to unlock it.

>! Any key opens any brig door. Keep your keys away from your prisoners.

A locked door opens only for the one who placed it, and ignores redstone. A prisoner standing in a closed space of at most 64 blocks, walled by solid blocks, bars and a locked brig door, is **in a cell** and does not try to escape, unless your crew's spirits sink so low that the watch grows careless.

## The three powers
The sea has three powers: the navy, the pirates and the merchants. Their quarrels rise and cool with every deed done at sea, and when the navy and the pirates are at each other's throats there are more patrols, more hunting and more raids.

Each power remembers you, from −100 to 100: the navy, the pirates and the villagers.

- Killing pirates pleases the navy. Killing navy men, plundering merchants and selling to fences wins the pirates over. Trading at villages pleases the villagers.
- Good standing gets you better prices; bad standing worse. Villagers stop trading with you at −60.
- Pirates leave you be once they like you well enough, until you strike first. Fall to −60 with the navy and it fires on sight.

## Navy patrols
The navy sends patrols between its outposts and out toward the pirate islands. A patrol keeps a lookout of some 256 blocks and gives chase to any ship under the Jolly Roger, any whose false colours it has seen through, and any whose captain is wanted or carries a bounty of 50 doubloons or more.

It circles you at about 20 blocks while its guns fire. Strike your colours and it falls silent. Outrun it and it gives up the chase.

## Careers
There are two ladders, and you may climb only one.

- **The navy.** Talk to a navy officer with an empty hand and enlist once the navy trusts you: a good name with it, no bounty and no friends among the pirates. Pirates killed, prisoners brought in, navy quests and a growing name promote you: Midshipman, Lieutenant, Captain, Commodore, Admiral. Firing on the navy or a merchant in service is desertion.
- **Infamy.** Plunder fenced, ships taken and a name among the pirates make you a Buccaneer, a Dread Captain and at last a Pirate Lord. A known pirate is never taken into the navy.
- **Letter of marque.** Not ready to serve? Buy one from a navy officer for 200 doubloons once the navy thinks well of you. Every pirate you kill under it earns prize money.

Rank has its rewards. A Lieutenant flies the navy flag by right, docks free at navy outposts and receives the officer's bicorne, a saber and the officer's coat, breeches and boots. A navy Captain receives a {item:pirates_n_ships:ship_commission}**ship commission** for an armed navy sloop; a Dread Captain is granted a pirate sloop at a pirate island's fence. A navy Captain may also order any ship at a navy outpost's desk for less than the villages ask.

Your title rides before your name in chat and over your head, and before your ship's name when you name her.
