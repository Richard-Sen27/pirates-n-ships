So you mean to go to sea. Good. Keep this book close, hand, for the sea forgives nothing twice and this Almanac holds what every captain before you learned the hard way: how to build a ship and sail her, how to keep a crew alive and willing, how to trade, how to fight, and how to keep clear of the hangman.

## Reading the Almanac
The {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_almanac}**Captain's Almanac** is bound from a book, two pieces of leather and two copper ingots.

- Use it in your hand to read it wherever you stand.
- Sneak and use it on a table or any surface to lay it down. A placed Almanac lies shut until someone opens it to read.
- Turn the pages with the corner arrows, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel. Escape closes the book.
- The ribbons on the edge are bookmarks, one per chapter. Hover one to read its name; click it to turn there.
- Words in {#2e6a24}green ink{/} are tips worth remembering, and words in {#9a1c14}red ink{/} are warnings you ignore at your peril.
- Names in {#2a4580}blue ink{/} lead to another chapter: click one to turn there.

The book remembers where you left off and opens there next time.

## Show me
Some pages carry a **Show me** button. Press it and the Almanac shows you the thing being done, step by step, on a little model of a ship. Back out of the scene and you return to the same page.

> Hover over an item that has a scene, such as the cannon, and hold the Ponder key to watch it from your inventory.

## How this book is laid out
Start with [[pirates_n_ships:first_ship|Your first ship]]: it takes you from a heap of planks to a ship under sail in a few pages. The chapters after it tell each part of the trade in full.

The last pages are for the purser and the quartermaster: [[pirates_n_ships:inventory|The ship's inventory]] lists every block and item with a line on each, [[pirates_n_ships:ledger|The Quartermaster's ledger]] holds the settings and orders a server's keeper needs, and [[pirates_n_ships:not_yet|Not yet charted]] owns up to what is still missing.
