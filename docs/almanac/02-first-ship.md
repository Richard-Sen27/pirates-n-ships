Every captain starts the same way: with planks, a helm and more courage than sense. Follow these steps in order and you will be under sail before the sun is down.

## 1. Lay the hull
show_me: ship_assembly | Show me: building and assembling a ship
Build a hull of wood floating in the water. Planks, slabs, stairs and glass keep the sea out; a hollow hull with a deck floats high and dry.

>! Leave a gap of one block to the shore, the quay or any other build. Everything that touches the hull becomes part of the ship.

Natural ground never goes to sea: dirt, sand, raw stone, ore, gravel, ice and leaves stay behind when the ship is launched. Cut and crafted blocks (cobblestone, bricks, logs and planks) sail with her.

## 2. Set the helm
Craft a {item:pirates_n_ships:helm}**helm** from four sticks and a plank and place it on the deck. Stand behind it and look the way she should sail: the bow is where the helmsman looks.

## 3. Rig a sail
Raise a mast of logs or fences. Across it set two rows of {item:pirates_n_ships:yard}**yards**, the lower one 2 to 8 blocks below the upper. Then place a {item:pirates_n_ships:sail_winch}**sail winch** anywhere on deck.

Add a {item:pirates_n_ships:capstan}**capstan** if you want an anchor, and you will.

## 4. Launch her
Use the helm. Every block joined to it becomes one ship: she floats, rolls with the sea, and keeps her hold dry.

## 5. Make sail
Use the sail winch: furled, then half, then full. The wind fills the canvas and she gathers way.

## 6. Steer
Hold right-click on the helm to take the wheel. Move the mouse, or hold A and D: right or D turns her to starboard, left or A to port. The line above your hotbar shows the rudder. Let go and the wheel stays where you left it.

## 7. Anchor
Use the capstan to let go the anchor, and again to weigh it.

## 8. Back to blocks
Sneak and use the helm with an empty hand to open the ship screen, then press **Disassemble** twice. She must lie nearly still and level first.

> That is the whole of it. Now read [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]] to learn what keeps her afloat, and [[pirates_n_ships:sailing|Sailing]] to learn how to make her go where you want.

## No hand for building?
A shipwright will build you a sloop. At a seafarer village, open the harbour master's desk and look at the **Orders** tab. Pay in doubloons, bring the logs and wool he asks for, and come back for her when she is ready. [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]] tells you more.
