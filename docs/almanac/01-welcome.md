So you mean to go to sea. Good. This book holds what every captain before you learned the hard way.

## Reading the Almanac
Lost yours? Bind a new {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_almanac}**Captain's Almanac**:

{recipe:captains_almanac}

- Use it to read; sneak and use it on any surface to lay it down.
- Turn pages with the corner arrows, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel. Escape closes it.
- {#2e6a24}Green ink{/} is a tip, {#9a1c14}red ink{/} a warning, {#2a4580}blue ink{/} a link to another chapter.

## Show me
Some pages carry a **Show me** button: the Almanac acts the thing out on a little model ship.

> Or hover any block or item a scene shows, such as the cannon or a railing of any wood, and hold the Ponder key.

## Where to start
Start with [[pirates_n_ships:first_ship|Your first ship]]: from planks to sail in a few pages.

At the back, [[pirates_n_ships:inventory|The ship's inventory]] lists every block and item, [[pirates_n_ships:ledger|The Quartermaster's ledger]] serves a server's keeper, and [[pirates_n_ships:not_yet|Not yet charted]] owns up to what is still missing.
