Buy where a thing is cheap, carry it where it is wanted, and sell it dear. That is the whole of honest trade, and a fine living it makes for a captain with a sound hold and a steady nerve.

## Crates and barrels
show_me: cargo_and_weight | Show me: stowing cargo
Bulk cargo goes in containers that hold **one kind of item** in great amounts.

- {item:pirates_n_ships:cargo_crate}**Cargo crate** (four planks and four sticks): 32 stacks of one item.
- {item:pirates_n_ships:cargo_barrel}**Cargo barrel** (six planks and two iron nuggets): 1536 items of one kind, whatever their stack size; best for rum and other small stacks.

Use one with a stack to put it in, with an empty hand to take a stack out, and sneak-use with an empty hand to put in everything of that kind you carry. Items that do not stack are refused, and stolen and clean goods of the same kind never share a container. A broken container keeps its cargo, and hoppers work on it.

## Weight and trim
Cargo weighs. Crates, cargo barrels, pantries and water barrels grow heavier as they fill: a full crate weighs as much as forty planks. A chest presses down where it stands, so a heavy chest in the bow trims her by the head.

>! Spread heavy cargo and keep it low and amidships. A laden ship sits deeper, floods sooner through a breach, and is slower to gather way and to turn.

The ship screen at the helm reads her load: Light, Laden, Heavily laden or Overloaded.

## The sea chest
illustration: sea_chest
The {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_chest}**sea chest** (three leather over iron, a chest and iron) holds 54 stacks and keeps them when broken, carried or floated.

- Use it in the air to carry it on your back. You cannot jump, sprint or swim, you walk slower, and in water it drags you under.
- Place it on water and it floats, drifting with wind and current.
- Use it to open it, sneak-use to pick it up, or strike it to knock it loose as an item.

A {item:pirates_n_ships:paddle}**paddle** (two planks and a stick) turns a floating chest into a boat of sorts: use the paddle on it to sit on the lid, then paddle forward and back and turn left and right. Sneak to get off. You cannot open the chest while you sit on it.

## Doubloons
illustration: doubloon
The gold {item:pirates_n_ships:doubloon}**doubloon** is the coin of the sea. Nobody mints it; you earn it:

- by selling goods at a harbour master's desk,
- from the purses of slain pirates and pirate captains,
- from the chests of wrecks and buried treasure,
- from bounties, ransoms, delivery contracts, quests and prize money.

Carry them in your inventory; that is where the markets take them from.

## Harbours
The coast has three kinds of port, each with a harbour master's desk.

- **Seafarer villages** stand on beaches: a stone quay with the harbour master's hut, a pier with two berths, cottages, a tavern and a shipwright's shed.
- **Navy outposts** are stone forts on beaches, rarer than villages: a sea gate, a quay with two berths, cannon on the walls and a garrison that never leaves its posts. An officer stands by the office door.
- **Pirate islands** are camps on beaches, rarer still: a jetty, tents, a tavern, a captain's hut and a fence's shack under the Jolly Roger. Strangers are not welcome.

A desk you place inside a port joins that port.

## The harbour master
illustration: harbor_desk
Every desk has a harbour master behind it, in a green frock coat and a peaked cap. Right-click him, or the {item:pirates_n_ships:harbor_desk}**harbour master's desk**, to open the port's market. If you strike him he waves you off for a while; if he dies, a new one takes the post after three days.

The desk's tabs:

- **Goods:** every good the port trades, with your doubloons. Green goods are made here and cheap to buy; orange goods are wanted here and good to sell. Pick a quantity, then Buy or Sell; the prices shown are for the whole quantity.
- **Contracts:** delivery offers and the contracts you hold.
- **Quests:** the port's errands; see below.
- **Orders:** the shipwright's slipway; see [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]].
- **Crew:** hands looking for a berth; see [[pirates_n_ships:crew|Crew]].

Stay within 8 blocks of the desk, or the market closes.

## Prices
Buying raises a price and selling lowers it, and prices drift back toward their usual level day by day. Buying and selling in one port always loses money; carrying a good from a port that makes it to a port that wants it pays, though less with every stack you sell.

The goods of the sea: sugar, fish, timber, iron, grain, hides, cocoa, gunpowder, {item:pirates_n_ships:tobacco}**tobacco**, {item:pirates_n_ships:spices}**spices**, {item:pirates_n_ships:cloth}**cloth** and rum. Spices fetch the most. Tropical ports grow sugar, cocoa, tobacco and spices; colder ports deal in fish, timber and iron; navy outposts sell powder.

Merchant convoys sail the lanes between the ports and move prices too: a port a convoy has just supplied pays a little less for what she brought.

## Stolen goods
Goods taken from a captured or sunk ship carry a plunder mark in their tooltip. Only a pirate fence buys them, at a heavy discount and with no questions. The desk's sell toggle chooses whether you sell clean or plundered stacks.

>! A village or navy harbour master turns plunder away and reports you to the navy. And the navy looks into your hold: sail within sight of its soldiers or a fort with more than 16 marked goods aboard, under any flag, and you are suspected of piracy. Fence your plunder before you go near the navy.

## Harbour dues
A navy outpost charges a small docking fee of 5 doubloons when your ship ties up at a berth or anchors inside it, once a day per ship. It comes from your purse, or from the coin in your ship's chests. Leave it unpaid and the outpost's desk will not trade with you until you settle it, coin in hand. Captains the navy trusts well, and officers from Lieutenant up, dock for free. Sailing through costs nothing.

## Contracts
A port offers delivery contracts: carry an amount of a good to another port by a deadline for a reward. Accepting costs a deposit of a fifth of the reward; delivering at the far port's desk pays the reward and gives the deposit back. You may hold three at a time.

## Quests
Every desk offers up to three quests, renewed every two days. Accept up to three at once; each must be done within five days.

- **Hunts:** pirates, sharks or, rarely, the kraken; or a pirate island's named captain.
- **Prisoners** (navy outposts): bring in pirates alive.
- **Cargo runs:** a contract with a raised reward and no deposit.
- **Treasure hunts:** you get a treasure map; open the chest at the X.
- **Escorts:** keep within 96 blocks of a merchant ship on at least half her course and see her into port.
- **Raids and patrols:** pirate islands want convoys plundered or navy patrols sunk; navy outposts want pirate ships sunk or taken.

A quest pays doubloons and raises your standing with whoever gave it.
