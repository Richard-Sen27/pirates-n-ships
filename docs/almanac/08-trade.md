## Stowing
- Use with a stack to put it in, with an empty hand to take a stack out.
- Sneak-use with an empty hand to put in everything of that kind you carry.
- A broken container keeps its cargo, and hoppers work on it.

Items that do not stack are refused, and stolen and clean goods never share a container.

## Weight and trim
Cargo weighs: containers grow heavier as they fill, and a chest presses down where it stands.

>! Keep heavy cargo low, spread and amidships. A laden ship sits deeper, floods sooner and is slower to turn.

The ship screen at the helm reads her load, from Light to Overloaded.

## The sea chest
illustration: sea_chest
The {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_chest}**sea chest** holds 54 stacks and keeps them when broken, carried or floated.

{recipe:sea_chest}

- Use it in the air to carry it on your back: slow, no jumping or swimming.
- Place it on water and it floats with wind and current.
- Sneak-use to pick it up; strike it to knock it loose.

## The paddle
Use a {item:pirates_n_ships:paddle}**paddle** on a floating sea chest to sit on the lid and paddle it like a little boat. Sneak to get off.

## Doubloons
illustration: doubloon
The gold {item:pirates_n_ships:doubloon}**doubloon** is the coin of the sea. You earn it by trading, from pirates' purses, wrecks and buried treasure, and from bounties, contracts and quests.

Keep them in your inventory; markets take them from there.

## Harbours
Every port has a harbour master's desk.

- **Seafarer villages:** a quay and a pier on a beach, then cobbled streets that bend and branch inland past cottages, taverns, a shipwright, a market and often a chapel or a beacon tower.
- **Navy outposts:** rarer stone forts with cannon and a garrison: a sea gate and quay, walls that bend inland round bastions and towers, and inside them lanes, barracks, a brig and a parade ground under the navy ensign.
- **Pirate islands:** rarer still, camps under the Jolly Roger: a rough jetty, then tents, huts and a tavern along winding trails up from the beach. Strangers are not welcome.

## The market
- **Goods:** buy and sell. Green goods are cheap here, orange goods wanted here.
- **Contracts** and **Quests:** see the next pages.
- **Orders:** the shipwright; see [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]].
- **Crew:** hands for hire; see [[pirates_n_ships:crew|Crew]].

Stay near the desk, or the market closes.

## Prices
Buying raises a price and selling lowers it; prices drift back day by day. Carry a good from a port that makes it to one that wants it.

Spices fetch the most. Tropical ports grow sugar, cocoa, {item:pirates_n_ships:tobacco}**tobacco** and {item:pirates_n_ships:spices}**spices**; colder ports deal in fish, timber and iron. {item:pirates_n_ships:cloth}**Cloth** you can weave yourself.

{recipe:cloth}

## Stolen goods
Goods from a captured or sunk ship carry a plunder mark. Only a pirate fence buys them, cheaply and with no questions.

>! Honest harbour masters report plunder to the navy, and a navy ship or fort that sees much of it in your hold suspects you of piracy.

## Harbour dues
A navy outpost charges a small fee when your ship berths or anchors there, once a day. Leave it unpaid and its desk will not trade with you. Trusted captains and officers dock free.

## Contracts and quests
A **contract** asks you to carry goods to another port by a deadline. You pay a deposit and get it back with the reward.

**Quests** pay doubloons and standing: hunts, prisoners, cargo runs, treasure hunts, escorts, raids and patrols. Each desk offers a few at a time, each with a deadline.
