An honest chart leaves blank what nobody has sailed. These are the waters this Almanac cannot yet describe, because nobody has built them yet.

## Still over the horizon
- **Oars** to move a big ship in a flat calm.
- **A ship's logbook** to record your voyages and deeds.
- **Paint for the hull,** to dye her planks your own colours.
- **A wanted poster** of your own, beyond the chat, the notice board and the rank box.
- **Feints of your own** with the sword, and a way to choose the side of your cut and your parry.
- **Sounds of their own** for most of the work aboard: until then, the creak of the hull, the shanties and the guns must do.

> If you find something in these pages that the sea does not do, or something the sea does that these pages do not tell, the mistake is the Almanac's. Tell whoever keeps your server.
