One captain cannot work the winch, the helm, the pumps and the guns at once. A crew can.

Hire hands, feed, pay and bunk them, and they sail her for you. The {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_whistle}**captain's whistle** gives the orders.

## Hiring hands
The **Crew** tab of a harbour master's desk lists who seeks a berth. Each asks a fee, then a daily wage.

- **Sailors**, at seafarer villages: 10 doubloons. Not for a captain the villagers refuse to trade with.
- **Navy ratings**, at navy outposts: 15. Enlisted officers only.
- **Pirates**, at pirate islands: 20. Only for a friend of the pirates, or a Buccaneer or worse.

Bring your ship within about 32 blocks of the port first: the new hand walks straight aboard.

>! Every hand needs a free {item:pirates_n_ships:hammock}**hammock**. The desk refuses a hand you cannot bunk.

## Stations
A station is a block a hand can work: the {item:pirates_n_ships:sail_winch}**sail winch**, the **helm**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:capstan}**capstan**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:bilge_pump}**bilge pump**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:cannon}**cannon**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:swivel_gun}**swivel gun** and the {item:pirates_n_ships:crows_nest}**crow's nest**.

A posted hand walks to his station, even under way, and stays there while she sails.

A hand with nothing to do strolls the deck, or leans on the {item:pirates_n_ships:oak_railing}**railing** a while and watches the sea. Any order calls him off.

## The captain's whistle
show_me: crew_orders | Show me: giving orders
- Use it on a hand, then on a station: he takes that station.
- Use it on a hand at a station: he is released.
- Sneak and use it on a hand: he is dismissed. Only you, or whoever hired him, may do so.
- Use it in the air: the order wheel opens. Click an order, or hold the use key, aim and let go.

The wheel: **Hoist**, **Reef** and **Furl sails**, **Man the pumps**, **Fire!**, **Load!**, **Fire at will**, the anchor, **Release crew**. The anchor is one order that switches: **Drop anchor** while it is stowed or coming up, **Raise anchor** while it is out.

> The ship screen at the helm gives the same orders. See [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]].

## Orders, not assignments
Blow an order and free hands on deck man the nearest unmanned stations that can carry it out. A hand you posted yourself is never moved.

If nobody can, you are told why.

**Release crew** sends everyone off and ends **Fire at will**.

## A helmsman
A hand at the helm steers the course he is given, but leaves the sails alone: order **Hoist sails** as well. Take the wheel and he lets go.

## Hammocks

show_me: hammocks_and_rest | Show me: slinging hammocks
Hang a hammock between two supports at the same height, such as posts, walls or the hull side: click the block next to one while looking toward the other.
{recipe:hammock}
At nightfall every hand off station turns in to the nearest free hammock. A night in one cheers him; a night aboard without one does not.

## Your own bunk
Right-click a free hammock at night to sleep in it as in a bed, even under way. Your respawn point sails with the ship.

> A {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_cot}**sea cot** is the captain's bed for the great cabin, and works the same way.

## Morale and wages
Every hand has a morale from 0 to 100; use the whistle on him to read it.

- A night in a hammock: up. Without one: down.
- Paid: up. Unpaid: down.
- Hungry or thirsty: down every day, and slower at work.
- Rum: up. Scurvy: down every day.

Every dawn each hand eats from the [[pirates_n_ships:provisions|Provisions]] and wants 2 doubloons, paid from the chests and crates aboard, then from your purse.

## Desertion
A hand below 20 morale for two dawns means to desert: he tells you, then walks off at the next port. Lift his spirits above 20 and he stays.

>! On some seas a miserable crew rises in mutiny and takes the ship. Your server tells you whether yours is one.

## Fire at will
On **Fire at will** your gun crews fire at the nearest hostile ship within 64 blocks. Your flag decides who is hostile:

- Navy flag: the Jolly Roger, and ships found out under false colours.
- Jolly Roger: navy ships, merchants and unflagged ships.
- Merchant flag or none: only the Jolly Roger.

Turn her to bring the guns to bear; see [[pirates_n_ships:combat|Weapons and combat]].
