A captain who does not know where he is soon knows where the rocks are. Keep your chart and mark it well.

## Your chart
Your {item:pirates_n_ships:chart}**chart** draws itself as you sail, inking in the coasts, shallows and open sea of the overworld around you. The land takes the colours of what stands on it, so a village's roofs and paths show up from afar.

{recipe:chart}

- Use it, or press **M** with it in hand, to open the map.
- Drag to pan; scroll to zoom, far enough out to see a whole sea.
- Right-click to place a named marker; click one to change it.

While you read it, you hold the chart unrolled between both hands, and every hand on deck sees you unroll it.

## Markers and landmarks
Mark as many places as you like. The **Markers** button lists them, nearest first, with their bearing; click one and the chart finds it.

Every port is on your chart from the start. Wrecks appear once you have sailed near them. Hover over an icon to learn what it is.

The red-tipped needle is you, pointing where you face; your ships are drawn with their bows.

> A chart shows only you and your own ships, never another captain or his ship.

## Map tiles
illustration: map_tile
Set {item:pirates_n_ships:map_tile}**map tiles** side by side on a wall or table, all facing one way, to make a board.

{recipe:map_tile}

## Drawing a board
1. Use a tile with your chart in hand.
2. Drag the frame over the waters you want and pick a zoom.
3. Press **Draw**.

Each tile costs an ink sac; one {item:pirates_n_ships:kraken_ink}**kraken ink** pays for eight. Anyone who has charted more can press **Update** to fill in the blanks.

## Reading bearings
The lookout calls points of the compass from the bow, 32 to the circle:

- **Dead ahead** and **dead astern.**
- **Two points off the starboard bow:** a little right of ahead.
- **On the beam:** square off the side.
- **Abaft the beam:** behind it, toward the stern.

## Treasure maps
illustration: treasure_map
Pirate fences sell blank {item:pirates_n_ships:treasure_map}**treasure maps**, and wrecks hold some. Use one and it marks the nearest unfound treasure.

Hold it to see the red X and the way there. At the X, dig into the sand for the chest.

> Pirate islands hide their chests under the sand: beneath two crossed logs, by a cairn, or among the ribs of an old boat.

## Wrecks
The ocean floor is strewn with wrecks, each with a chest of ship's stores: doubloons, rum, supplies, now and then a treasure map. Tall wrecks lie in deep water. Bring something to breathe and see down there.
