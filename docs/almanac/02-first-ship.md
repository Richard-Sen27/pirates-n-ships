Every captain starts with planks, a helm and more courage than sense. Follow these steps.

## 1. Lay the hull
show_me: ship_assembly | Show me: assembling a ship
Build a wooden hull floating in the water.

>! Leave a one-block gap to the shore and every other build. Whatever touches the hull becomes part of the ship.

Natural ground (dirt, sand, raw stone, leaves) stays behind; cut and crafted blocks sail with her.

## 2. Set the helm
Place a {item:pirates_n_ships:helm}**helm** on deck. The bow is where the helmsman looks.

{spotlight3d:block:helm}

{recipe:helm}

Helms come in every wood, a wood mod's too, and so do the winch, the capstan, the bilge pump and the cannon's carriage: each is made from that wood's planks, and the panels show each wood in turn.

## 3. Rig a sail
Raise a mast of logs or fences with two rows of {item:pirates_n_ships:yard}**yards** across it, 2 to 8 blocks apart.

{recipe:yard}

Add a {item:pirates_n_ships:sail_winch}**sail winch** on deck, and a {item:pirates_n_ships:capstan}**capstan** for an anchor.

{recipe:sail_winch}

## 4. Launch and make sail
Use the helm: everything joined to it becomes one ship. Use the winch to set sail: furled, half, full.

## 5. Steer
Hold right-click on the helm and turn with the mouse or A and D.

## 6. Anchor and unmake
Use the capstan to drop or weigh anchor.

To unmake her, sneak and use the helm with an empty hand and press **Disassemble** twice.

> Now read [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]] and [[pirates_n_ships:sailing|Sailing]].

## No hand for building?
A seafarer village's shipwright builds to order: a sloop (300 or 400 doubloons) or a two-masted brig (725) with a gun deck pierced for eight guns, three holds, a cabin and four square sails and a jib. See the **Orders** tab of the harbour master's desk, and [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]].
