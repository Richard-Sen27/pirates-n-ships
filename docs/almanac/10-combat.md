## Swordplay
- **Slash:** a quick left click. A wide, short arc.
- **Thrust:** hold left click a moment, then release. Narrow, longer and harder, but slow to recover if it misses.
- **Guard:** hold right click. It stops every blow from the front, at a cost in stamina. Blows from behind or the side get through.
- **Parry:** tap right click just before a blow lands. The attacker staggers and you may **riposte** for extra harm.

>! While you hold a sword, right click belongs to the blade. Put it away to open a door or take the helm.

## Stamina and your foe
A brass-framed bar shows your stamina. Attacks, guarding and missed parries drain it; rest refills it. Empty, you can neither guard nor parry.

Pirates fight as you do. Parry when the arm goes up or back, but beware the feint that baits an early parry.

## Loading and firing
1. **Load:** with shot and powder in your pack, hold right click: three seconds for the pistol, five for the musket.
2. **Aim:** hold right click. After a second of steady aim the shot tightens.
3. **Fire:** left click. From the hip the ball scatters wide.

Letting go of right click never fires. Sneak to lower an aimed gun.

>! In the rain a quarter of your shots misfire; the charge stays in.

## The cannon

Click the deck where the **front** of the carriage should stand, by the rail: the barrel overhangs one block ahead. Both halves need a block beneath them.
{recipe:cannon}

> A cannon cannot turn sideways. Lay your ship so her broadside bears.

## Working the gun
1. **Load:** gunpowder first, then a {item:pirates_n_ships:cannonball}**cannonball**.
2. **Aim:** sneak and use it with an empty hand. The upper half raises the barrel, the lower half lowers it.
3. **Fire:** use it with an empty hand.

{recipe:cannonball}

## Shot and holes
The barrel needs five seconds to cool. A ball smashes the wood it strikes, and a hole below the waterline lets the sea in. Hit square; a grazing ball glances off.

## Chain shot and grapeshot
Both load like a ball, after the powder. {item:pirates_n_ships:chain_shot}**Chain shot** breaks no hull but tears sails, cuts ropes and breaks ratlines. Torn cloth mends slowly by itself.
{recipe:chain_shot}
{item:pirates_n_ships:grapeshot}**Grapeshot** bursts into a cone of pellets that hurt men and break nothing. Sweep a crowded deck before you board.

## Gun crews
Post a hand at a gun with the whistle and stock powder and shot in a chest, barrel or crate within 4 blocks on the same ship. Blow **Load!**, then **Fire!**: every manned, loaded gun fires, and the crew reloads it.

**Fire at will** lets the crews pick targets; see [[pirates_n_ships:crew|Crew]].

## The grappling hook
illustration: grappling_hook
The {item:pirates_n_ships:grappling_hook}**grappling hook** bites into ships and land. Right click to throw it, with 32 blocks of rope. Or hold it in your off hand, load it into a musket and fire it, with 64 blocks.
{recipe:grappling_hook}

## On the rope
- **Two ships:** hooked from your own ship, the rope hauls them side by side.
- **Haul:** hold sneak with the rope in hand and walk back.
- **Climb:** right-click your own rope when the hook is two blocks or more above you.
- **Make fast:** use a cleat or mooring ring of yours while the hook is out.
- **Let go:** sneak and use with an empty hand.

It slips off water, leaves, glass panes and creatures.

## Boarding
show_me: boarding | Show me: boarding an enemy
Use a rope made fast to a cleat or ring to slide down it, from your masthead onto her deck.

With the ships alongside, use a {item:pirates_n_ships:boarding_plank}**boarding plank** on top of your gunwale. Keep the ships together or it breaks.
{recipe:boarding_plank}

## Taking a prize
Merchantmen, navy patrols and pirates sail the sea lanes.

- Take goods from her hold while aboard: plundered.
- Sink her with the last shot: the deed is yours.
- Kill every fighter and hold her deck: she is yours, crew and all.

>! Taking a merchant is piracy. See [[pirates_n_ships:law|Law]].

## Foes ashore and afloat
illustration: mob_pirate
- **Pirates** attack you and the navy on sight.
- **Navy soldiers** shoot at you only when you are wanted.
- **Navy officers** are deadly with the saber.
- **Sailors** never fight.

Striking navy men is a crime; killing pirates is not.

## Pirate captains
illustration: pirate_captain
Every pirate island has a captain with a bounty of 300 doubloons. Sneak and use him with a sword in hand to call a duel; his crew keeps out of it.

Slain, he drops his hat, a treasure map and your bounty proof.

## Raids
Linger at a navy outpost or seafarer village and pirates may raid it. Kill every raider to beat them off.

## Monsters of the deep
illustration: kraken
The **kraken** rises beside ships in the deep ocean, most often at night and in storms. It drags hulls down and smashes masts. Strike its eyes.

It leaves a {item:pirates_n_ships:kraken_beak}**kraken beak** and {item:pirates_n_ships:kraken_ink}**kraken ink**.

## Sharks
illustration: shark
Sharks hunt swimmers, and blood drives them into a frenzy. Stay on deck or in a boat and they leave you alone.

## Waterspouts and whirlpools
In a storm at sea a **waterspout** pulls in and lifts what comes near, and tears set sails. Steer clear, or furl first.

In the deep ocean a **whirlpool** drags boats and swimmers under and turns ships about.

> Big ships barely notice either.
