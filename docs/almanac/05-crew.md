One captain cannot work the winch, the helm, the pumps and the guns at once. A crew can. Hire good hands, feed them, pay them and give them a dry place to sleep, and they will sail your ship for you.

## Hiring hands
Every harbour master's desk has a **Crew** tab. Each day a port has a few souls looking for a berth: sailors at seafarer villages, pirates at pirate islands, navy ratings at navy outposts. Each asks a one-time fee and then a daily wage.

- A sailor asks 10 doubloons, a navy rating 15, a pirate 20.
- Villagers will not sign on with a captain they refuse to trade with.
- Pirates sign on only with a friend of the pirates or a captain of some infamy, a Buccaneer or worse.
- Navy ratings sign on only with an enlisted officer.

Before you hire, bring your own ship in and lie within about 32 blocks of the port. The new hand walks straight aboard and waits on deck.

>! Every hand needs a free {item:pirates_n_ships:hammock}**hammock**. Hang more hammocks before you take on more crew; the desk refuses a hand you cannot bunk.

## Stations
A station is a block a hand can work: the {item:pirates_n_ships:sail_winch}**sail winch**, the **helm**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:capstan}**capstan**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:bilge_pump}**bilge pump**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:cannon}**cannon**, the {item:pirates_n_ships:swivel_gun}**swivel gun** and the {item:pirates_n_ships:crows_nest}**crow's nest**.

A hand at a station stays at his post while the ship sails and turns. He walks there across the deck, even under way, and the station starts its work only when he has arrived. If he cannot reach it, or has not got there after ten seconds, he is put there anyway.

A hand leaves his station when it is broken, when the ship is taken apart, or when he dies.

## The captain's whistle
show_me: crew_orders | Show me: giving orders
The {item:pirates_n_ships:captains_whistle}**captain's whistle** is how a captain speaks to his crew.

- Use it on a hand, then on a station: he takes that station and stays there.
- Use it on a hand at a station: he is released.
- Sneak and use it on a hand to dismiss him from your service. Only you, or whoever hired him, may do so.
- Use it in the air to open the order wheel. Point at an order and click, or hold the use key, aim and let go. Escape closes the wheel.

The orders on the wheel: **Hoist sails**, **Reef sails**, **Furl sails**, **Man the pumps**, **Fire!**, **Load!**, **Fire at will**, **Drop anchor**, **Weigh anchor** and **Release crew**.

> No whistle in your sea chest? The ship screen at the helm gives every one of these orders too, and assigns, releases and dismisses hands. See [[pirates_n_ships:ships|Ships]].

## Orders, not assignments
You need not post every hand yourself. Blow an order and every unmanned station that can carry it out becomes an open job. Free hands on deck take the nearest one by themselves within a second, get to work and stay there afterwards. A hand you posted yourself stays put and is never moved.

A hand answers in chat ("Aye, hoisting the sails!") and then works. Each step of trim takes him two seconds; the sails change when the work is done.

If nobody can carry an order out, you are told why: no ship under you, no station aboard that takes it, nothing to do, or what is wrong with the rig. If every hand is busy you hear "No free hands", and the job waits for the next hand who comes aboard.

**Release crew** sends every hand off his station, ends **Fire at will** and cancels the open jobs.

## A helmsman
Post a hand at the helm and he keeps her steady. He turns the wheel toward the course he is given and puts the rudder midships when he arrives ("We've arrived, captain! Rudder midships"). He does not touch the sails: order **Hoist sails** as well.

Take the wheel yourself at any time and he lets go; release it and he steers on. If the ship stops making headway with her sails set, he tells you she is stuck.

## Meals
Twice a day, at noon and at sunset, the hands who are off duty sit down by the {item:pirates_n_ships:pantry}**pantry** (or the water barrel) and eat for a few seconds. An order gets them up at once.

The meal is a sight to see, nothing more: the crew's rations are taken once a day, at dawn. What they eat and drink, and what happens when the pantry runs dry, is in [[pirates_n_ships:provisions|Provisions]].

## Hammocks and rest
illustration: hammock
show_me: hammocks_and_rest | Show me: slinging hammocks
Hang a {item:pirates_n_ships:hammock}**hammock** between two supports at the same height, such as fence posts, walls, logs or the hull side: click the block next to one support while looking toward the other.

At nightfall every hand who is not at a station turns in to the nearest free hammock and gets up at dawn. A night in a hammock lifts his morale; a night aboard without a free hammock lowers it, and he will grumble. Hands on watch all night are not affected, and an order at night gets sleepers up at once. Each hammock is one bunk.

You may sleep in one too, on land or aboard: right-click a free one at night and you lie in it as in a bed. Aboard you stay in it while she sails, you count toward skipping the night, and the dawn puts you on your feet beside it. Sleeping there sets your respawn point, and aboard that point sails with the ship.

> The captain sleeps in a {item:pirates_n_ships:sea_cot}**sea cot**, a wooden bed for the great cabin, which works the same way at sea.

## Morale
Every hand has a morale between 0 and 100; a new hand comes aboard at 70. You read it on the ship screen, or by using the whistle on him.

- A night in a hammock: a little up. A night without one: down by ten.
- Paid on time: up by one. Unpaid: down by eight.
- Hungry: down by ten a day. Thirsty: down by twenty-five a day.
- A ration of rum: up a little each day.
- Scurvy: down every day until it is cured.

Hungry hands work at three quarters of their speed, thirsty hands at half.

## Wages and desertion
Every dawn each hand wants 2 doubloons. The pay comes from the chests, barrels and cargo crates aboard, the ones nearest the helm first, and the lowest in spirits are paid first. If the coin aboard runs short, the rest comes out of your own purse while you are at sea in that world.

A hand whose morale stays below 20 for two dawns means to desert. He tells you so and walks off at the next port the ship comes within 48 blocks of, or wherever she is after three more dawns. Feed him, pay him and lift his spirits above 20 again, and he changes his mind.

>! On some seas a whole crew that stays miserable for three dawns rises in mutiny, turns pirate and takes the ship from you. Your server tells you whether yours is such a sea.

## Fire at will
Order **Fire at will** and your gun crews take over their cannons. Each crew lays its gun on the nearest hostile ship within its arc and 64 blocks, leads a moving target, loads from the powder and shot nearby and fires when the shot will tell.

Who is hostile depends on your own flag:

- Under the navy flag: the Jolly Roger, and ships whose false colours are found out.
- Under the Jolly Roger: navy ships, merchants and unflagged ships.
- Under a merchant flag or none: only the Jolly Roger.

Nobody fires on a ship that has struck her colours, and your crew holds fire if you strike yours. A cannon cannot turn sideways, so turn the ship to bring the guns to bear. **Release crew** ends it. The guns themselves are in [[pirates_n_ships:combat|Weapons and combat]].
