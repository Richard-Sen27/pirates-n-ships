# Release notes 1.0.0

**Maintainer notes (not part of the published notes).**

- **Format.** `tools/release_notes.py` writes notes from the commit subjects between two tags, as `## Features`,
  `## Fixes`, `## Art and sounds` and `## Other` sections of `- ` lines; the release workflow puts one loader line
  above it in `build/release-notes.md` and publishes that file to Modrinth, CurseForge and GitHub. These notes use the
  same Markdown: `##` sections and one `- ` line per entry, so they read the same next to the generated part.
- **Why a hand-written text for 1.0.0.** There is no earlier tag, so `python3 tools/release_notes.py "" v1.0.0` lists
  every `feat` and `fix` commit since the start (over 450 lines of work-package subjects). For 1.0.0 the published
  notes are the loader line, then the text below "PUBLISHED NOTES", then a link to the full history instead of the
  generated list. From the next release on the generated notes are short again, and a hand-written
  `docs/release/<version>-notes.md` is optional (highlights above the generated list).
- **How to combine them.** The workflow does not read this file yet. The change it needs in
  `.github/workflows/release.yml`, step "Release notes", replacing the line `python3 tools/release_notes.py "$PREVIOUS" "$REF"`:

  ```sh
  notes="docs/release/${VERSION}-notes.md"   # VERSION: steps.version.outputs.version, passed in env
  if [[ -f "$notes" ]]; then
    sed -n '/^PUBLISHED NOTES$/,$p' "$notes" | tail -n +2   # the hand-written text below the marker
  fi
  if [[ -n "$PREVIOUS" ]]; then
    echo; python3 tools/release_notes.py "$PREVIOUS" "$REF"   # the generated list, only after an earlier tag
  fi
  ```

  Preview locally the same way: `sed -n '/^PUBLISHED NOTES$/,$p' docs/release/1.0.0-notes.md | tail -n +2`.
- **Checks.** `python3 tools/check_release_texts.py` checks the item and block names (italics), config keys and the
  dependency versions below against the generated lang and `gradle.properties`. Every highlight was checked against
  `docs/progress.md` on 2026-10-10; a line for something that is not playtested by release day comes out.
- **Save and config notes** point at `docs/release/save-compatibility.md` (SAV1), which says what a pre-release world
  keeps and loses.

PUBLISHED NOTES

**Pirates 'n' Ships 1.0.0**, the first full release: real block ships that float, flood and sink, sailed by the
wind and worked by a crew, in a sea of ports, pirates, navy patrols and monsters. Every feature has a server config
switch.

## Ships and sailing

- Build a ship from any blocks and launch her at the *Helm*; sneak-use the helm to turn her back into blocks.
- Dry hulls below the waterline; breaches, flooding between compartments, listing and sinking.
- Fight a leak with the *Bilge Pump* and the *Hull Patch*; rejoin a hull shot in two with the *Shipwright's Toolkit*.
- A world-wide wind from the weather; square sails between *Yards*, triangular sails on *Cleats*, set at the *Sail Winch*.
- Steer by turning the wheel; ships float level as built and heel in a gust.
- The anchor on a chain at the *Capstan*, the *Mooring Ring*, *Oars* and the *Oak Oar Bench*.
- Visible waves that rock ships, wind streaks, a wake; a ship HUD and a ship screen at the helm.
- *Name Boards*, figureheads, tall flagpoles, the *Crow's Nest*, *Ratlines*, railings with gun ports, *Hammocks*.
- Dyed sails and banners on large sails; cargo weight that trims the ship; sloops built to order by shipwrights.

## Crew

- Hire hands at the *Harbor Master's Desk*, order them with the *Captain's Whistle*.
- Crew hoist, reef and furl, pump, load and fire, row, work the anchor, hold a course, keep a lookout and board.
- Wages, rations, water, rum, morale, scurvy, desertion and mutiny.

## Combat

- Swordplay with the *Rapier*, *Cutlass* and *Saber*: slash, thrust, guard, parry, riposte, stamina.
- The *Pistol* and the *Musket*.
- The two-block *Cannon* with *Cannonballs*, *Chain Shot* and *Grapeshot*; the *Swivel Gun* on the rail.
- The *Grappling Hook*, rope hauling and sliding, the *Boarding Plank* and the "Board!" order.

## The world

- Seafarer villages, pirate islands with buried treasure, navy outposts and wrecks.
- Factions, convoys on sea lanes, navy patrols, pirate raids, NPC ships you can fight, plunder and capture.
- Markets with moving prices, contracts, harbour dues, fences, *Treasure Maps* and quests.
- *Sharks*, the *Kraken*, waterspouts and whirlpools; cold water, rum, the *Sea Chest* and the *Paddle*.
- Your own *Chart* of every coast you pass, and *Map Tiles* that join into boards.

## The law

- Criminal score, bounties, the *Bounty Proof* and the *Notice Board*.
- *Shackles*, the brig, turn-ins, fines, ransom and the press-gang.
- Flags and false colours, striking colours; navy ranks, pirate infamy, the letter of marque, reputation.

## The Captain's Almanac

- The in-game guide, given on first join: index, bookmarks, recipe panels and Show me scenes.
- JEI information pages when JEI is installed.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, Java 21.
- NeoForge 21.1.228 or newer, or Fabric with Fabric Loader 0.19.3, Fabric API 0.116.12+1.21.1 and Forge Config API Port 21.1.3 or newer.
- Sable 2.0.6 (below 3.0), GeckoLib 4.9.3, Player Animation Library 1.1.6+mc.1.21.1 or newer.
- Bundled in the jar: Ponder 1.0.87 and Flywheel 1.0.6.
- Optional: JEI 19.39.0.372 or newer.

## Worlds and config from a pre-release

- What a world from a 0.x or a dev build keeps and loses under 1.0.0 is listed in
  [save-compatibility.md](https://github.com/Richard-Sen27/pirates-n-ships/blob/main/docs/release/save-compatibility.md). Back up the world first.
- An existing config file keeps its old values; new settings get their default. To take every 1.0.0 default,
  delete `pirates_n_ships-server.toml` in `config` (or in the world's `serverconfig` folder if you put a copy there)
  and `pirates_n_ships-client.toml` in `config`; they are written again on the next start. Settings that no longer
  exist are removed from the file (the old file is kept as a `.bak`), and values outside a new range are clamped.

## Known limits

- Most of the work aboard has no sound of its own yet.
- A sword in hand takes the right mouse button (guard and parry): swap items to use doors or the helm.
- Only the NPC duelists feint; no directional attack and parry mode.
- Chain shot does not tear triangular sails; the *Swivel Gun* takes neither chain shot nor grapeshot.
- Railings, bulwarks and oar benches in oak only; no logbook, hull paint or wanted poster yet.
- Fabric needs Mod Menu for the config screen.
- JEI is held at 19.39.0.372 until the mod moves to a newer NeoForge.
